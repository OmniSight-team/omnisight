## OMNI-901 · K-20: fix the UCA-timed UCF-Crime windows

### Why this ticket exists

K-20: in OMNI-609's hand check, UCA-timed windows were wrong twice as often as official ones, mostly by containing
the event. The forecaster (OMNI-903 onward) trains on these windows, so they are fixed before any training.

### What was done

2026-11-23.

- **Looked for the fix's room first.** Every UCF-Crime train and validation positive window is UCA-timed (460 + 80
  per length); all 140 official-timed ones are held-out. So K-20's "train on official only" is impossible, and the
  ticket's recommended fix (a fixed margin) is the one taken
- **Sized the margin from annotations only.** The hand-check notes in `event_windows.verify_reason` give how late
  the late UCA Wrongs were: Robbery006 2.8 s, Arrest011 8.9 s, Stealing043 40.6 s. The OMNI-603 comparison (110 test
  videos with both times) gave how often UCA falls inside the event past a margin: 18 at 0 s, 14 at 3 s, 11 at 5 s,
  8 at 8 s. The cost, from the committed windows: 3 s drops 103 of 540 windows, 8 s drops 182. Chose **8 s** (D-49):
  the smallest whole-second margin that clears Arrest011 under D-27's 1.5 s tolerance
- **Code:** `omnisight_common.datasets.windows`: `UCA_MARGIN_S = 8.0`; a UCA-timed positive ends 8 s before the UCA
  time and keeps its length; flag `uca_margin` (flags are now `;`-separated); new skip reason for UCA events under
  8 s into the video. `build_event_windows.py --replace` rewrites the CSVs once, leaves `event_windows` alone, and
  prints old and new hashes
- **A detour:** the hand-check sample's redraw test broke, because 182 sampled-pool clips lost their windows. Added
  `hand_check.windowed_clips` (positive now, or skipped for the margin) which is exactly the OMNI-607 pool; the
  sample redraws unchanged
- **Re-check prepared, not done:** `scripts/uca_recheck.py draw` wrote `datasets/hand_check/uca_recheck.csv`, 15
  UCA clips (seed 901, every one of the 13 classes, plus a second Abuse and Arrest), none from OMNI-609. The
  criterion is in D-49, written before any clip is watched. UCF-Crime is no longer loaded in the product, so the
  clips are watched from the Drive copy

### Verification performed

- `test_event_windows.py`: the margin, the flag in the CSV, official and CCD windows without margin, the new skip
  reason; the byte-for-byte rebuild test against the new CSVs. `test_hand_check_sample.py` and
  `services/query/tests/test_windows.py` pass (27 tests)
- Rebuilt file against the committed one: 2,180 of 2,180 held-out and negative rows identical; 716 of 716 kept UCA
  rows end exactly 8 s earlier and carry the flag; `ccd.csv` unchanged (same hash)

### Not verified

- The full test suite after the change (only the affected files were run)

## OMNI-902 · Colab: clip vectors for every training window (UCF-Crime part)

### Why this ticket exists

The PFM reads a window of clip vectors (Algorithm 4, step 1); only the VIRAT archive had them. CCD's part was done

### What was done

2026-11-25, the code; the Colab run is next.

- The same runner, `scripts/encode_pfm_windows.py`: UCF-Crime's step is 1 s (D-54), and **no context before a
  window** (`context_s = 0`): a sample's sequence starts at its window's start (D-55), so nothing before it is read

## OMNI-903 · Training samples from the windows (UCF-Crime part)

### Why this ticket exists

The PFM trains on samples: a sequence of clip vectors ending at `t`, its class and, on a positive, `τ`. CCD's were
built in OMNI-X16 (`docs/context/extra-pfm-encoder-ab.md`); UCF-Crime's waited for its vectors (OMNI-902).

### What was done

2026-11-25. The same builder, `scripts/pfm_samples.py build ucf_crime`: every 1 s grid point inside each 30 s and 60 s
window, from the window's start (D-55); a UCA-timed window's event is 8 s after its end (D-49).
`datasets/samples/ucf_crime.csv`: 96,172 samples (counts in `results.md`). Two fixes on the way:

- **No sample on the event's own first frame.** 32 positive samples (16 videos, both lengths) had `τ` = 0.0003 s:
  the event starts on a frame (Arson022: frame 3500, 116.6667 s), the window's end is rounded to the millisecond
  (116.667), so the grid's last point was the event's first frame, against D-24's "never any part of the event".
  The builder now drops a positive grid point less than half a frame before the event; the window's other points
  stay. CCD's file is byte-identical (its smallest `τ` is 0.1 s)
- **`Store` read a sequence as consecutive rows of its video's file.** True on CCD (one window per video), not on
  UCF-Crime: a normal video's 30 s and 60 s windows sit at independent positions, so the video's grid interleaves
  their points (the first check failed on `Normal_Videos002`: frames 563 and 593 with 578 between). `Store` now
  looks up each frame of the sequence (evenly spaced, from `start_frame` to `end_frame`)

### Verification performed

- `scripts/tests/test_pfm_samples.py`, 2 new tests (8 in all): a window whose rounded end falls after the event's
  first frame loses only that point; a 30 s window interleaved with a 60 s window in one file reads its own points.
  With the model's tests, 15 pass
- `check ucf_crime` against Drive's `embeddings/pfm/ucf_crime/internvideo2/`: all 96,172 samples map to grid vectors,
  82,777 vectors of 512-D, norm 1; every sample in its video's split, all 1,448 videos present

### Not verified

- Training on them (OMNI-905)

### Result

**Done.** Both domains have their samples: CCD 35,161, UCF-Crime 96,172. Two things for OMNI-905 to decide: Normal is
77% of UCF-Crime's samples, and 7,200 samples are the same under both lengths (short videos). Validation is thin for
some classes (Shooting has 1 video).

## OMNI-905 · Train both domains; thresholds on validation

### Why this ticket exists

The PFM is one model for both domains (Algorithm 4: a shared backbone, a head per domain). OMNI-904 proved it learns
on CCD alone; this trains it on CCD and UCF-Crime together, chooses it and its thresholds on validation, and records
the run so the product can load it (OMNI-910).

### What was done

2026-11-26, the code; the Colab run is next. The rules first, in D-59, before any training.

- `scripts/train_pfm.py`: `train(..., validate=...)` measures validation after every epoch and keeps the best epoch
  in `best.pt` (its epoch and score also in `last.pt`, so a resumed run chooses the same way); `load()`; `evaluate`
  also gives top-3 over the positive samples (SM-2's measure)
- `scripts/pfm_model.py`: `Config.alphas`, a `Δt` weight per domain. **Found on the dry run:** the first loss was
  133, almost all UCF-Crime's `Δt` error in seconds squared (its `τ` reaches 67 s; D-56's `α = 1` was set on CCD's,
  under 5 s). `α_d = 1 / var(τ)` of the train positives (D-59)
- `scripts/pfm_runs.py`: the run record (`run.json` beside the weights on Drive, `model_runs.json` listing the runs and
  the chosen one) and `download`, which copies the chosen run into `models/trained/pfm/` and checks its SHA-256
- `notebooks/pfm_train_joint_gpu.ipynb`: copies both domains' InternVideo2 folders from Drive, trains 3 seeds with
  validation after every epoch, chooses, sets `θ_d`, writes the record and `results/omni905_joint.json`

### Verification performed

- `scripts/tests/test_pfm_model.py` (3 new: the best epoch kept, a tie not replacing it, and a resumed run choosing
  the same; top-3; a per-domain `α`) and `scripts/tests/test_pfm_runs.py` (2: record, register, download, a changed
  checkpoint refused). With the samples' and the encoder's tests, 29 pass
- **Dry run of the notebook's own cells on this laptop's CPU**, real vectors from Drive (16 videos per domain, train

## OMNI-908 · The rule detector

### Why this ticket exists

Slice 9's stand-in for the model: two deliberately simple rules fill the inbox OMNI-907 built, so the workflow
(raise, review) is real before the PFM exists.

### What was done

  its grid size; `HeartbeatGate` passes the inner gate's mask on. Carried through `SegmentRecord`, `SegmentMessage`,
  the extraction response, the consumer's insert and re-gating. No decision changes
- **Tables** (migration `d5f2b8c4a6e1`): `segments.motion_cells` (BIGINT), `segments.motion_grid`; on `cameras` the
  zone (4 fractions, checked inside the frame), `sustained_motion_s` (10 s) and `active_from` / `active_until` (both or

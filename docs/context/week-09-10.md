## OMNI-901 · K-20: fix the UCA-timed UCF-Crime windows

### Why this ticket exists

K-20: in OMNI-609's hand check, UCA-timed windows were wrong twice as often as official ones, mostly by containing
the event. The forecaster (OMNI-903 onward) trains on these windows, so they are fixed before any training.

### What was done

2026-11-23.

- **Looked for the fix's room first.** Every UCF-Crime train and validation positive window is UCA-timed (460 + 80
  per length); all 140 official-timed ones are held-out. So K-20's "train on official only" is impossible, and the
  ticket's recommended fix (a fixed margin) is the one taken
- **Sized the margin from annotations only.** The hand-check notes in `event_windows.verify_reason` give how late
  the late UCA Wrongs were: Robbery006 2.8 s, Arrest011 8.9 s, Stealing043 40.6 s. The OMNI-603 comparison (110 test
  videos with both times) gave how often UCA falls inside the event past a margin: 18 at 0 s, 14 at 3 s, 11 at 5 s,
  8 at 8 s. The cost, from the committed windows: 3 s drops 103 of 540 windows, 8 s drops 182. Chose **8 s** (D-49):
  the smallest whole-second margin that clears Arrest011 under D-27's 1.5 s tolerance
- **Code:** `omnisight_common.datasets.windows`: `UCA_MARGIN_S = 8.0`; a UCA-timed positive ends 8 s before the UCA
  time and keeps its length; flag `uca_margin` (flags are now `;`-separated); new skip reason for UCA events under
  8 s into the video. `build_event_windows.py --replace` rewrites the CSVs once, leaves `event_windows` alone, and
  prints old and new hashes
- **A detour:** the hand-check sample's redraw test broke, because 182 sampled-pool clips lost their windows. Added
  `hand_check.windowed_clips` (positive now, or skipped for the margin) which is exactly the OMNI-607 pool; the
  sample redraws unchanged
- **Re-check prepared, not done:** `scripts/uca_recheck.py draw` wrote `datasets/hand_check/uca_recheck.csv`, 15
  UCA clips (seed 901, every one of the 13 classes, plus a second Abuse and Arrest), none from OMNI-609. The
  criterion is in D-49, written before any clip is watched. UCF-Crime is no longer loaded in the product, so the
  clips are watched from the Drive copy

### Verification performed

- `test_event_windows.py`: the margin, the flag in the CSV, official and CCD windows without margin, the new skip
  reason; the byte-for-byte rebuild test against the new CSVs. `test_hand_check_sample.py` and
  `services/query/tests/test_windows.py` pass (27 tests)
- Rebuilt file against the committed one: 2,180 of 2,180 held-out and negative rows identical; 716 of 716 kept UCA
  rows end exactly 8 s earlier and carry the flag; `ccd.csv` unchanged (same hash)

### Not verified

- The re-check by eye (the ticket's third item): waits for Ahmad
- The full test suite after the change (only the affected files were run)

### Result

UCF-Crime windows rebuilt: 358 UCA-timed positives per length (was 540), official and negatives unchanged. Re-check
sample drawn; its result decides whether K-20 closes.

## OMNI-907 · Alerts: the table, the API and the roles

### Why this ticket exists

Slice 9's inbox (FR-6, FR-9, NFR-7). The rule detector (OMNI-908) and later the PFM (OMNI-911) raise alerts;
this ticket stores them and lets people review them. Started before OMNI-902 because Colab was unavailable and
this needs no model.

### What was done

2026-11-23.

- **Read first:** the plan's alerts section (`implementation-plan.md`: the columns, `status` new / confirmed /
  dismissed, `source` rule / model, the four routes) and Phase A (§3.5.5: "an estimated severity attached",
  no scale). Four things neither says were decided in D-51: severity `low` / `medium` / `high`; "unread" is
  `status = new`; a review is final (409 on a second); assigning only to an active operator or analyst
- `omnisight_common.alerts`: `AlertStatus`, `AlertSource`, `AlertSeverity`, shared because `pfm` will raise
  alerts and `query` serves them
- **Table** (`Alert`, migration `c3e8a1f5b7d2`): the plan's columns plus `video_id` and `assigned_to`. Checks:
  exactly one of camera or video; a reviewed status has its time. Keys: video CASCADE, camera none (as
  segments), segment / reviewer / assignee SET NULL
- **API** (`app/alerts.py`): `GET /alerts` with `status`, `camera_id`, `video_id`, `severity`, `source`,
  `sort=newest|soonest`, `limit`; each alert carries its camera or video name, the frame's moment and
  thumbnail, and the reviewer's and assignee's emails, for the page (OMNI-909). `POST /alerts/{id}/confirm`,
  `/dismiss`, `/assign {user_id}`, `GET /alerts/unread_count`. The alert row is locked while it is decided
- `/alerts` and `/alerts/unread_count` added to TC-10's list of protected routes
- **Detours:** the CHECK first tied the review to `reviewed_by` too, which a deleted reviewer (SET NULL) would
  have broken; it ties to `reviewed_at` only. A test fixture read ids after its session closed (teardown
  errors only, every test had passed); fixed

## OMNI-908 · The rule detector

### Why this ticket exists

Slice 9's stand-in for the model: two deliberately simple rules fill the inbox OMNI-907 built, so the workflow
(raise, review) is real before the PFM exists.

### What was done

2026-11-23.

- **Algorithm 1 records its moved cells** (D-52): `MotionGate` keeps lines 4-5's boolean cell array as a bitmask with
  its grid size; `HeartbeatGate` passes the inner gate's mask on. Carried through `SegmentRecord`, `SegmentMessage`,
  the extraction response, the consumer's insert and re-gating. No decision changes
- **Tables** (migration `d5f2b8c4a6e1`): `segments.motion_cells` (BIGINT), `segments.motion_grid`; on `cameras` the
  zone (4 fractions, checked inside the frame), `sustained_motion_s` (10 s) and `active_from` / `active_until` (both or
  neither). The camera API shows them as `rules` and `PATCH` sets them (a bad zone 422; `active_hours: null` clears)
- **`services/pfm`** (it was an empty folder): `pfm/rules.py` (the two rules as plain functions), `pfm/detector.py`
  (each look reads every enabled camera's last 2 minutes of admitted live frames, follows a run back to its start,
  skips a run that already has its alert, inserts the rest), `pfm/main.py` (the loop on a thread, `GET /health`), a
  Dockerfile, its dependencies, and `pfm` in `docker-compose.yml` with no host port
- **A mistake found on the real feed:** both rules first used one pause, 2 s. On `cam08` "activity outside hours"
  raised **11 alerts in about 40 s**: the clip's motion comes in 1-1.5 s bursts 7-15 s apart (read from the stored
  frames), each burst a new run. The outside-hours rule now ends a run after 60 s without motion, and a heartbeat no
  longer ends a run by itself (it is a gap anyway). Re-run: 1 alert in 90 s
- **Found on the way, not fixed** (K-34): after restarting four services at once, nginx sent `/api` to ingestion
  (stale address for `query`); restarting nginx fixed it

### Verification performed

- `services/ingestion/tests/test_motion_cells.py` (5): the mask names the cells that moved and its size equals the
  score; a discarded frame keeps its mask, warm-up has none; the bottom-right cell fits a signed BIGINT; over 64 cells
  no mask; a heartbeat carries the inner mask. The gate's existing tests pass unchanged
- `services/pfm/tests/test_rules.py` (13): zone by cell centre, local hours and spans over midnight, runs split only
  at a long pause, sustained fires once at N, shorter or outside the zone or heartbeats only do not, outside hours is
  one alert per stretch of activity. **The ticket's two clips:** a short synthetic clip with a block moving in the
  left half and a still one, through ingestion's own gate as a live camera stamps them: the moving clip raises
  sustained motion (left-half zone, N = 5 s), the still clip keeps only heartbeats and raises nothing, and the moving
  clip with the zone on the still right edge raises nothing
- `services/pfm/tests/test_rule_detector.py` (7, on the test database): one alert with its frame, severity and reason;
  the same run never twice, after a restart (a new detector) or as it goes on; a run longer than the window is
  followed back to its start; a new run after a pause raises again; outside the zone, too short or heartbeats only
  raise nothing; outside hours only where hours are set; a disabled camera is not watched
- **On the running stack** (migration applied, `pfm` built and up, `/health` ok, a look every 2 s), with simulator
  cameras registered through the API by a throwaway admin, captured, then stopped and disabled:
  - `cam08` (UCF-Crime with an incident), hours set to exclude now: **1** "Activity outside hours" alert for 90 s of
    bursty activity, `high`, with its thumbnail and *motion at 14:43:42, outside the camera's hours 17:43-18:43*;
    every stored frame (290) has its mask, grid 8
  - `cam08`, N = 1 s, 60 s: 68 motion frames in 4 runs, 1 lasting at least 1 s; **exactly one alert, on that run,
    none on the others**
  - Must not: N = 3600 s and hours off, 45 s more capture on two cameras: **0** new alerts
  - `cam01` (VIRAT): 76 frames, **no motion frame** in 90 s (heartbeats only, K-23), so no alert, as expected

# Sprint 9–10 (merged) — Alerts, and the model that predicts them

**Slices 9 and 10 · Mon 23 Nov – Tue 1 Dec 2026 · branch `slice-09-10-forecast`**
Board: OmniSight Phase B · Assignee: Ahmad

> **By Tue 1 Dec:** a trained forecasting model (Algorithm 4) with SM-2 and SM-3 measured on
> held-out data, and an Alerts page where alerts are raised, confirmed, dismissed and
> assigned: first by simple rules, then by the model, naming the expected event and how long
> until it. This is what the supervisor sees on Wed 2 Dec.

**Covers:** FR-5 · FR-6 (part) · FR-9 (alerts) · NFR-3 · NFR-7 (new routes) · SM-2 · SM-3 ·
TC-5 (first half)
**Algorithm:** Algorithm 4, the PFM (Phase A §3.5.4, Appendix B.4): a shared temporal
Transformer over a window of clip vectors, one head per domain, predicting the event type and
the time until it; confidence `κ = 1 − H(p) / log|C_d|`; an alert when `max(p) ≥ θ_d`.
Loss `CE + α · MSE(Δt)`, summed over the domains with weights `λ_d`.
**Ships in:** `notebooks/` · `scripts/` · `services/pfm/` · `services/query/` · `frontend/` ·
`docker-compose.yml`

## Why two slices in one

The meeting with the supervisor is on Wed 2 Dec. Slice 9 (the alerts inbox on rules) and
slice 10 (the model replaces the rules) are **one sprint that ends Tue 1 Dec**. Each ticket
has a day.

takes GPU hours, so it starts on day one and runs in the background while the Alerts page is
built. Each day pairs a Colab ticket with a product ticket.

## OMNI-904 · The PFM model, and proof that it can learn · Wed 25 Nov

- [x] Algorithm 4 as written: a small Transformer encoder over the window (shared by both
      domains), a head per domain giving `p` (the classes) and `Δt`; `κ` from the entropy
- [x] The loss of Appendix B.4: `CE + α · MSE(Δt)` (MSE only on positives), summed over the
      domains with `λ_d`. Every setting (layers, width, `α`, `λ_d`) in one config, saved with
      the checkpoint
- [x] **Overfit proof:** on a tiny train subset (e.g. 32 samples) the loss goes near zero
- [x] **Resume proof:** stop mid-training, restart, and it continues from the checkpoint on
      Drive with the same loss curve
- [x] Unit tests for the shapes, the mask, `κ`, and the loss, on the laptop's CPU

## What to read this sprint

- [Attention Is All You Need](https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.03762) — the Transformer reads a
  sequence of moments, not a sentence.
- [Saving and loading models](https://pytorch.org/tutorials/beginner/saving_loading_models.html)
  — Colab sessions end; training must resume, and serving loads once.
- [The CCD paper: uncertainty-based accident anticipation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2008.00334) — how CCD's results are
  usually reported.
- [Threshold selection](https://scikit-learn.org/stable/modules/model_evaluation.html) — set
  on validation, never on the test data.
